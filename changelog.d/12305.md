---
synopsis: Start packages on the critical path first in parallel builds
packages: [cabal-install]
issues: 12305
---

When more packages are ready to build than there are job slots,
`cabal build -j N` (with or without `--semaphore`) now starts the package
heading the costliest chain of packages still to build, estimating each
package's cost by its number of modules. Previously, packages started in the
order they became ready, so a long chain of dependencies could wait behind
many small packages. Serial builds (`-j1`) keep their order.
